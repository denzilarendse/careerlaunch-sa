APPLICATION SAFETY

Protect your documents and money

  • Use the official source linked on the opportunity card.
  • Do not pay an application fee unless the official provider explicitly requires one.
  • Do not send passwords, PINs or OTPs with an application.
  • Keep reference numbers and submission confirmations for your tracker.
